Space in No Man's Sky used to have a simple threat model: the occasional pirate ambush, easily pulsed away from. The **Swarm update** (6.4, May 2026) complicated the sky. Swarmer ships that attack in coordinated groups now make routine flights feel like a battle, pirate freighters can be beaten and *boarded* for control, and the update's expedition added the Hive of Glass encounter on top. This guide covers the fights themselves — what each battle is, and the kit that wins them.

**Last updated: August 22, 2026**, current through patch 6.50. For the expedition's structure and rewards, see our [Expedition 22 guide](/expeditions/expedition-22/); this page is the combat companion.

## What the Swarm Update Changed

Three layers arrived together in May 2026:

1. **Swarmers** — hostile ships that fight as a swarm: multiple coordinated attackers rather than the lone-pirate model, raising the skill floor of ordinary space travel.
2. **Pirate freighter boarding** — pirate capital ships can now be defeated and boarded; rather than scrapping the wreck, you demand control of the freighter. Space battles gained a prize worth fighting for.
3. **Expedition 22: The Swarm** — the time-limited campaign around the new threat, run as a first-of-its-kind three-team war effort from the Nexus, culminating in the **Hive of Glass** boss encounter.

The distinction that matters: the first two layers stayed in the game; the expedition layer is seasonal, returning in [redux reruns](/expeditions/redux-expeditions-schedule/).

## Fighting Swarmers

Swarmer combat is pirate combat with the volume turned up. Where a pirate wing attacks in twos and threes and breaks off, a swarm sustains: more ships, coordinated approach, pressure that punishes a ship built for single duels.

The tactics that hold up:

- **Keep moving, keep turning.** Swarms reward ships that can hold speed through turns; drifting in a straight line invites every attacker at once. The classic pirate dogfighting loop — boost, turn, snap shots during the merge — remains the core motion.
- **Kill the closest, not the biggest.** Damage that arrives is damage from the nearest attacker; thin the closest wingmate and incoming pressure drops immediately.
- **Manage shields as a resource.** With multiple attackers, shield recharge windows matter more than raw damage; disengage upward or downward, let the recharge tick, and re-engage.
- **Engine and weapons first.** For ship module priority, sustained combat favors weapon and shield upgrades over cargo or scanner comfort — the [upgrade rules](/multitools/multitool-upgrades/) for adjacency and module limits apply to ship grids too.

## Beating and Boarding Pirate Freighters

The boarding mechanic is the update's structural change to capital-ship combat. The flow:

1. **Win the battle.** A pirate freighter fight is an endurance engagement — survive the swarm escort, then work the freighter itself, turrets first, engines to pin it.
2. **Board the beaten ship.** With the freighter disabled, dock and board rather than flying off.
3. **Demand control.** The Swarm update's addition: confront the beaten crew and demand they hand over the freighter. The battle converts to a capital-ship prize rather than a debris field.

For players building toward a fleet, this turned pirate battles from a chore into a hunting loop — the mechanics of capital freighter ownership are covered in the [freighter guide](/starships/freighter-guide/), and the farming side in [capital freighter farming](/starships/capital-freighter-farming/).

## The Hive of Glass

The expedition's centerpiece encounter is boss content: a set-piece fight at the climax of the Swarm campaign, fought alongside the three-team community war effort that defined Expedition 22's structure. As with all expedition content, its availability follows the [expedition schedule](/expeditions/expedition-guide/) — active during the original run, returning in reduxes. If it is live when you read this: bring the sustained-combat kit above, expect a longer fight than a pirate engagement, and check the expedition's own phases in the [Expedition 22 guide](/expeditions/expedition-22/).

## The Kit That Wins Swarm Fights

| Slot | Priority | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| Ship type | Fighter-class or a strong combat roll | Dogfight endurance over cargo ([best ships](/starships/best-ships/)) |
| Weapons | Upgraded photon cannon / phase beam line | Sustained damage for sustained waves |
| Shields | Shield modules, adjacency-grouped | Survivability is the swarm bottleneck |
| Engine | Pulse and boost upgrades | Escape-and-recharge cycles |
| Multitool | Boltcaster or neutron cannon line | Ground-side pirates and dissonance cross-overs ([weapon modes](/multitools/weapon-modes-guide/)) |

The Swarm-era consensus on difficulty: it made space *honestly* dangerous without making it punishing — a mid-game ship with disciplined modules handles swarms; a starter ship gets chased. If you are skipping every fight, the fix is upgrades, not avoidance.

## After the Swarm

The expedition ended on schedule, but the sky stayed changed: swarmer encounters and freighter boarding are now permanent texture, and the update's generous rewards — the Direwasp armor set, a new multitool, and the usual cosmetic haul — remain claimable in redux reruns. For what is live right now, the [expeditions schedule](/expeditions/redux-expeditions-schedule/) tracks the calendar, and the current flagship content is the [Cosmos update](/cosmos-update/cosmos-update-guide/) — the anniversary update that followed Swarm by a season and changed travel instead of combat. The sky got meaner in May; the galaxy got bigger in August. Both are worth a loadout slot.
