A decade in, No Man's Sky has quietly become several games wearing one spacesuit: the mining-and-building sandbox, the 2026 creature-battling layer, the meaner post-Swarm sky, and a Cosmos-flavored anniversary overhaul still settling into place. Whether you never left or are landing for the first time since the Atlas Rises era, the 2026 edition rewards knowing which tricks still work, which systems are new, and how the three big updates changed the answers. Thirty tips, organized by world.

**Last updated: August 22, 2026**, current through patch 6.50. For first-hours basics see [starter tips](/guides/starter-tips/); this page is the everything-after companion.

## The 2026 Content Layer

1. **Do the Oceanus daily.** The Xeno Arena's daily challenge is the best-designed 15 minutes in the 2026 content — one puzzle, worldwide, every day ([pet battling guide](/guides/pet-battling-guide/)).

2. **The warping galaxy reset doubles the daily.** Community-verified: clearing the daily's once-per-day limit via a galaxy-center warp resets Oceanus for another fight. The 30-victory milestone crowd swears by it.

3. **Retroviral Pellets are a plan, not a snack.** Edit your roster's abilities deliberately — one great stat creature with a patched moveset beats six wild rolls.

4. **Swarmers made the sky honest.** Post-Swarm, routine flights can turn contested ([swarm battles](/guides/swarm-battles-guide/)). Upgrade ship shields before engines before cargo — the old pirate kit, now with endurance mattering.

5. **Board the freighter, don't scrap it.** Beaten pirate freighters can be boarded and *commandeered* since Swarm — the biggest mechanical gift to combat-minded players in years.

6. **Cosmos rolls out in stages.** The anniversary update is publishing mechanics piecewise — interstellar free flight is announced and arriving in waves, so treat any 2026 guide's specifics (including ours) as point-in-time and check the [Cosmos hub](/cosmos-update/cosmos-update-guide/) for the current state.

7. **Keep warp cells even in the free-flight era.** Free interstellar flight is an option beside warping, not a replacement — the efficiency answer stays the hyperdrive ([interstellar guide](/cosmos-update/interstellar-flight-guide/)).

8. **Landing on stars is real now.** The Cosmos update made stars approachable destinations for the first time — the how-to is [here](/cosmos-update/how-to-land-on-stars/), and it changes photography, refueling, and bragging rights equally.

## Economy and Progression

9. **Three modules per family — still the law.** A fourth same-family upgrade module does nothing, in 2026 as in 2018. The discipline never expires ([upgrades explained](/multitools/multitool-upgrades/)).

10. **Adjacent placement is free performance.** Cluster modules around their core tech; the stat shift is visible live in the install screen. Most "bad rolls" are actually scattered grids.

11. **The scanner is still the best first investment.** Three S-class scanner modules turn every planet walk into income — the early-game advice that survived every update ([units farming](/guides/units-farming/)).

12. **Build one indium farm, retire the grind.** An activated indium hotspot with extractors pays passively forever; every other money loop is what you did before ([the farm guide](/guides/units-farming/)).

13. **Salvaged data funds your whole base catalog.** Dig buried modules whenever the visor shows them — ninety seconds each, permanent part unlocks ([parts unlock](/base-building/base-parts-unlock/)).

14. **Same-numbered storage containers share inventory.** Container 4 is one box across every base and your freighter — the logistics trick that makes a teleporter network into a supply network ([storage guide](/base-building/storage-containers/)).

15. **Nanites are a shopping problem, not a farming problem.** Modules to buy, scrapping discipline, mission counters — the loop is in the [nanites guide](/guides/nanites-farming/).

## Ships and Combat

16. **Wealthy three-star systems for everything.** Ships, multitools, module stock, prices — every wall in a rich system is better stocked ([the hunting method](/starships/s-class-ships/)).

17. **Save before you shop.** Station stock rerolls on reload; a manual save turns any disappointing cabinet into a free re-roll.

18. **Crashed ships beat early purchases.** Free slot upgrades for repair materials — the starter economics that hold forever ([starter tips](/guides/starter-tips/)).

19. **The dissonance loop is deterministic.** Want a great multitool without spawn luck? Echo Locator to harmonic camp, every time ([sentinel multitool](/multitools/sentinel-multitool/)).

20. **Neutron cannon for the swarm era.** The no-ammo weapon line ages well as fights get longer — charge discipline beats ammunition logistics in sustained waves ([weapon modes](/multitools/weapon-modes-guide/)).

## Base and World

21. **Survey before the Base Computer goes down.** Power and mineral hotspots decide everything a base can be, and the computer cannot be moved — only deleted ([placement guide](/base-building/base-computer-placement/)).

22. **EM hotspots end the power question.** One strong electromagnetic hotspot runs a megabase forever; solar-and-battery is the honest starter grid ([electrical wiring](/base-building/electrical-wiring-guide/)).

23. **Bases are never raided.** Build on any horror planet in confidence — Sentinels respond to your actions, not your architecture ([planet picking](/base-building/best-base-planets/)).

24. **The portfolio beats the palace.** Per-base object limits mean a home, a farm, a power outpost, and a dissonance camp outperform one megabase — and a freighter base travels.

25. **Freighter rooms mirror storage numbers.** The same-numbered containers aboard your capital ship make the freighter the mobile half of your logistics ([freighter guide](/starships/freighter-guide/)).

## The Meta-Tricks

26. **Ride the expedition calendar.** Expeditions remain the highest reward-density content in the game, and 2026's cadence has been generous — [check the schedule](/expeditions/expedition-guide/) before planning any grind.

27. **Cross-save makes the Switch 2 a real option.** With [crossplay and cross-save](/guides/crossplay-guide/) mature, platform is now a preference, not a commitment — the [Switch 2 performance](/guides/switch-2-performance/) holds up.

28. **The wiki habit compounds.** Systems like [refining](/guides/refining-recipes/) and [expedition planning](/expeditions/expedition-fast-completion/) pay every session; learn each once, benefit forever.

29. **Take the screenshot.** Photo mode is the actual endgame content, and post-Cosmos the skies have never been more worth it.

30. **Play in seasons.** The mature 2026 loop is: new update lands, ride it hard for two weeks, settle into your projects, return for the next. Ten years in, Hello Games has earned the trust that the next drop is worth the wait — and the [Cosmos rollout](/cosmos-update/cosmos-new-features/) is the current proof.
