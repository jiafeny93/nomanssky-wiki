## How Heat Protection Works in No Man's Sky Right Now

Your exosuit's Hazard Protection is the first line of defense against extreme heat. It drains continuously while you are exposed to a hot environment, drains much faster during firestorms and other extreme weather events, and regenerates over time once you reach shelter or leave the hazardous area. Standing inside your starship, a building, a cave, or any enclosed structure will stop the drain and start the recovery.

Protection can be augmented with upgrade modules. The core item for heat is the Thermal Protection Module, which you can buy as upgrade modules from technology dealers on space stations. These modules come in classes from C up to S, with S-class rolls offering the strongest protection and drain resistance. The critical rule: you can install up to three protection upgrades of the same type in your exosuit. Install a fourth and all of them shut down until you remove one, which is one of the most common mistakes newer travelers make.

## The Best Heat Resistant Gear You Can Install Today

If you are farming extreme heat planets for Storm Crystals or hunting for the perfect scorched-world base site, this is the loadout most veteran players run. No Man's Sky heat resistant gear is less about one overpowered item and more about stacking the right combination cleanly.

| Gear | Where to Get It | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal Protection Module (Heat) | Space station technology dealers | The core heat shield upgrade; buy several and keep the best rolls |
| Stacked heat modules (up to 3) | Same dealer, reroll by buying more | Three is the hard limit; a fourth disables the whole set |
| X-class illegal hazard upgrades | Outlaw space stations | Offer stronger or unusual protection stats at a trade-off |
| Supercharged exosuit slots | Unlocked as you expand your exosuit | Placing heat modules adjacent to these boosts their output |

A few practical notes. S-class modules are worth the wait, so check the technology dealer's stock every time you dock at a new station. X-class upgrades bought at outlaw systems can roll higher protection than legal modules, but their stats are inconsistent, so inspect each one before installing it. Finally, keep your heat modules together in one inventory area and place the strongest ones in or next to supercharged slots for the largest benefit.

## Surviving Extreme Heat Planets: Field Technique

Gear only carries you so far. On the hottest worlds, technique is what keeps you alive through a firestorm.

| Situation | Best Counter |
| --- | --- |
| Firestorm incoming | Get underground or indoors; caves stop hazard drain completely |
| Hazard protection empty mid-storm | Recharge with Sodium or shielding items, then move to shelter |
| Long trek across a scorched biome | Keep your starship within summoning range as a mobile shelter |
| Farming during storms | Time harvests between storm cycles and unload before the next one |
| Sustained presence on a hot world | Build a small base with teleporter as a permanent recharge point |

One habit worth building now: glance at the weather indicator whenever you land. Extreme heat worlds cycle between calm windows and storm surges, and doing your surface work in the calm windows roughly doubles how long your heat resistant gear lasts before you need to recharge or retreat.

### What is the best heat protection available today?

Three Thermal Protection Module upgrades for heat, ideally S-class, installed together in your exosuit with the strongest ones adjacent to supercharged slots. Add X-class illegal hazard upgrades from outlaw stations if you want to push further, but inspect their stats carefully because rolls vary widely.

### How many heat protection upgrades can I install?

You can install up to three protection upgrades of the same type. Installing four or more of the same type disables all of them until you dismantle enough to get back under the limit, so never add a fourth heat module to your suit.

### How do I recharge hazard protection in the field?

Sodium and crafted shielding items refill your hazard protection bar, and any enclosed shelter, including your starship, stops the drain and lets protection regenerate on its own. Carrying a stack of Sodium is the cheapest insurance on a scorched world.

### Will my existing thermal gear become obsolete after Cosmos?

Almost certainly not. Heat hazards on planets are a core survival loop, and the new star-landing technology has been presented as a new tier of heat resistant gear rather than a replacement. Expect your current modules to keep working, with the new equipment adding capability on top. We will confirm exactly how the two systems interact once the full patch notes are published.
