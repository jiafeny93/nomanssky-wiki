No Man's Sky has two economies. The one you can see — units, ships, shopping — and the one that runs underneath: refining. Every serious unit or nanite income in the game passes through a refiner at some point, because refiners convert the cheap and abundant into the scarce and valuable while you are off doing something more interesting. The game currently ships with **358 refining recipes** in its data, and almost all of them are intermediate steps. The ones with real leverage fit on this page.

**Last updated: August 31, 2026**, current through patch 6.50. The staged Cosmos rollout has not touched any recipe below. For what the money numbers mean, see [money making](/guides/money-making-guide/); for nanites end to end, [nanites farming](/guides/nanites-farming/); for the farm loops that feed off these tables, [units farming](/guides/units-farming/).

## The Three Refiners

| Refiner | Power | Inputs | Role |
|---------|-------|--------|------|
| Portable | Carbon fuel slot | 1 input → 1 output | The field tool — deploy anywhere |
| Medium | Base power | 2 input slots | The oxygen-catalyst loops |
| Large | Base power | 3 input slots | Factory throughput |

The portable refiner is a backpack appliance: craft it, drop it anywhere, feed it carbon, and it works through your input while you mine the next deposit. Medium and large refiners are base infrastructure — they need a powered base ([the electrical guide](/base-building/electrical-wiring-guide/) covers that side) — and their extra slots unlock the real recipe book, because the strongest conversions **use oxygen as a catalyst**, and those all need two inputs.

Two mechanical notes before the tables. Refiners have **input capacity limits per slot** — load in batches and top up as they consume. And refiners **pause, not fail**: out of fuel or power they simply stop, contents intact, and resume when fed. A large refiner runs every recipe on this page; a portable only runs the one-input rows.

## The Multiplier Loops

These are the recipes the whole refining economy hangs on. Each one returns more than it consumes, which means a stocked input grows every cycle:

| Recipe | Output | The play |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Chlorine x1 + Oxygen x2 | Chlorine x6 | The chlorine expansion loop — the classic unit printer |
| Ionised Cobalt x1 + Oxygen x2 | Ionised Cobalt x6 | Same loop, cobalt flavour — cobalt is in every cave |
| Condensed Carbon x1 + Oxygen x2 | Condensed Carbon x6 | Free fuel, sixfold — ends carbon mining forever |
| Carbon x2 + Oxygen x2 | Condensed Carbon x5 | The entry version, before you hold a stock |
| Salt x2 + Oxygen x2 | Chlorine x5 | Makes starter chlorine from beach salt |
| Cobalt x2 + Oxygen x2 | Ionised Cobalt x5 | Same, for cobalt |
| Di-hydrogen x30 | Di-hydrogen Jelly x1 | Half of the di-hydrogen cycle… |
| Di-hydrogen Jelly x1 | Di-hydrogen x40 | …and the other half: 30 in, 40 out, +10 free launch fuel per cycle |
| Platinum x1 + Oxygen x1 | Magnetised Ferrite x10 | The platinum trick — the best ore multiplier in the game |
| Any ore x1 + Chromatic Metal x1 | Ore x2 | Chromatic expansion duplicates copper, cadmium, emeril, indium, quartzite |
| Any plant x1 + Kelp Sac x1 + Condensed Carbon x1 | Oxygen x10 | Artificial photosynthesis — the refiner farms its own catalyst |

The common thread: **oxygen is the catalyst of the entire economy**. Every x5/x6 loop burns it, so the real setup cost is an oxygen supply — a hotspot, the red oxide plants that grow everywhere, or kelp sacs refined with anything. Since stations stopped selling the stuff, the loops that farm their own oxygen are the ones that survive. What the output sells for per hour is the [units farming](/guides/units-farming/) question; the recipe never changed.

## Gases: The Exchange and the Components

The three atmospheric gases interconvert freely, which means no gas is ever the wrong one:

| Recipe | Output | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Any gas x3 | Next gas x1 | Sulphurine → Radon → Nitrogen → Sulphurine, in a wheel |
| Any gas x1 + Oxygen x1 | Next gas x1 | Oxygen halves the cost of the exchange |
| Any gas x1 + Chromatic Metal x1 | Next gas x1 | The chromatic-catalyst version |
| Any gas x100 + Carbon x20 + Salt x10 | 1 component | Enriched Carbon, Thermic Condensate, Nitrogen Salt — the craft-chain components |

That last row is where refined gases leave the refiner and enter the crafting economy: one gas per trade good, consumed by the high-end craft chains in the [money making guide](/guides/money-making-guide/).

## Turning Recipes Into Units

Refining alone is a decent income. Refining **feeding crafting** is the real one: raw materials → refined components → crafted trade goods, each step multiplying value. The three classic loops all ride the tables above:

1. **The chlorine loop** — salt to starter chlorine, then the x6 expansion until the refiner bank is full; sell in three-star systems.
2. **The chromatic ladder** — blue-star ore into chromatic metal, chromatic expansion to double the ore, ore into metal again.
3. **The component factory** — gas collectors feeding sublimation components feeding the stasis-device chains.

The consistent theme: never sell what a refiner can upgrade first. Raw ore sells as raw ore forever; the same ore walked two steps down a recipe chain sells as technology. The full strategy, with the farm-base setups that feed it, is in [money making](/guides/money-making-guide/).

## Building the Refining Base

The refinery base has a shape, and it is the same for everyone:

- **A powered room** — medium and large refiners on the grid ([power guide](/base-building/electrical-wiring-guide/)).
- **Storage adjacency** — inputs and outputs beside the machines, in the [numbered containers](/base-building/storage-containers/): ores in one number, refined goods in the next.
- **A teleporter at the door** — the base is a stop on your loop, in and out in minutes.
- **An oxygen hotspot nearby** — the catalyst for every multiplier loop on this page, piped in through supply pipes if you can.
- **Optional: cultivation** — frost crystal and other growable inputs within walking distance of the refiners.

Three large refiners, a teleporter, and a full storage wall is the entire factory, and it fits in one cuboid room. The returns compound for the rest of the save.

## Rules of Thumb

- **Never idle a refiner at your base** — power is free once the grid is up; an empty refiner is an unused income.
- **Refine before selling, always** — the two-step rule that separates rich saves from busy ones.
- **Batch, top up, collect** — respect the slot limits; top up inputs as they drain rather than babysitting.
- **Collect before you log off** — refiners pause safely, but the habit removes the risk entirely.
- **Run the multiplier loops before the mine runs out** — a x6 loop doubles down on itself; a deposit does not.

Refining is No Man's Sky's quiet answer to "how do I afford all of this": a system that works while you don't. Set up the room once, keep the machines fed, and the unit and nanite problems quietly solve themselves in the background of every expedition.
