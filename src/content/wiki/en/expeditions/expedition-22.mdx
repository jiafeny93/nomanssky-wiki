## What Was Expedition 22: The Swarm?

The Swarm expedition launched alongside The Swarm update (version 6.4), the largest space-combat overhaul No Man's Sky had seen. Where most expeditions are a solo checklist, The Swarm framed its milestones as a **galaxy-wide war**: you flew out, fought the swarms the update's enemies spawned, and reported back to the anomaly. The centerpiece was the **Hive of Glass** boss encounter, and the update's new swarmer enemy ships made even routine flights feel like a battle.

The expedition also split the community into **three teams** competing in a shared war effort launched from the Nexus — a structure the game had never used before. Your milestones fed your team's overall progress, which made the run feel less like a checklist and more like a season.

## Expedition 22 Rewards

The rewards for The Swarm leaned into the update's insectoid enemy theme:

- **Insectoid Armor set** — the expedition's signature cosmetic, matching the Swarm's chitin-and-glass enemies
- **Swarm Expedition decal** — a profile emblem for completing the run
- **Phase rewards** — the usual expedition mix of cosmetics and titles across each phase

Like every expedition, anything you earned can be claimed on other saves from the **expedition terminal on the Space Anomaly** — rewards are account-wide once unlocked.

## How to Replay Expedition 22 Now

The limited-time window is over, but the expedition is not gone:

1. **Expedition terminal on the Space Anomaly** — past expeditions are replayable from the terminal, so you can still work through The Swarm's milestones at your own pace
2. **New game start** — beginning a fresh save offers the option to start any previously released expedition
3. **Watch for a Redux re-run** — Hello Games traditionally replays past expeditions in the fall (late October is the community's usual expectation), when The Swarm would return as a limited-time Redux with the same reward track

If you are chasing the Insectoid Armor specifically, the terminal replay is the reliable route — Redux re-runs are announced with little lead time.

## Is There an Expedition Running Right Now?

As of mid-August 2026, **no limited-time expedition is active**. Expedition 21 (Remnant) ran February 11 to March 13, Expedition 22 (The Swarm) ran May 27 to July 19, and the community is waiting on expedition news tied to the **Cosmos tenth-anniversary update** announced on August 9. When a new run is confirmed, this page and our [expedition guide](/expeditions/expedition-guide) will be updated the same day.
