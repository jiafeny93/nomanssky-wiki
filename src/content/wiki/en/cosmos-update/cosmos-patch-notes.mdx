## What Is the Cosmos Update?

Cosmos is the summer 2026 update and the centerpiece of No Man's Sky's tenth anniversary celebrations. The game launched on August 9, 2016, and the anniversary announcement arrived alongside an open letter from Hello Games co-founder Sean Murray, a tenth anniversary trailer, and coverage from essentially every major gaming outlet. Reporting around the announcement also noted that the game set new player count records during the celebration.

The framing from Hello Games is that this update begins a "brand new era" for the game. After ten years of free content drops that added base building, freighters, exocraft, settlements, mechs, pets, living ships, and far more, Cosmos reaches past planetary content and reworks how you move through the galaxy itself.

## How Cosmos Fits the 2026 Update Line

Cosmos caps an unusually busy year for the game. February brought the Remnant update and Expedition 21. May delivered The Swarm, with its fleet-scale space combat and Expedition 22. June added companion creature battling and was followed by patches 6.36 and 6.45.1 with fixes. Each of those updates followed the same release pattern Cosmos is following now: announcement, staged details, then full notes on the release log at launch.

If you are coming back for Cosmos after time away, the short version is that space combat, ground combat, pets, and expedition rewards all received meaningful attention earlier in 2026, and your existing save carries all of it forward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does Cosmos release?

Hello Games has announced Cosmos as its summer 2026 anniversary update. The complete patch notes and final release specifics are still being published as of August 16, 2026, so watch the official release log rather than third-party dates. ### Is the Cosmos update free?

Every one of the more than forty major updates in No Man's Sky's ten-year history has been free, and the anniversary letter emphasizes that record. No paid expansion has been announced for Cosmos.

### Do I keep my save when Cosmos launches?

Yes. No Man's Sky updates have always applied to existing saves, and nothing in the announcement suggests a reset. Your bases, ships, freighter, and progress carry forward.

### Will Cosmos change how warping between systems works?

Flying directly to another solar system is a confirmed feature, so interstellar travel is changing in a fundamental way. Exactly how the new flight model interacts with the existing warp system has not been itemized in official notes yet. That detail will be added here once published.

### Where should I report bugs after the update?

Use the official bug reporting form linked from the No Man's Sky website. Hello Games historically ships hotfixes quickly after major updates, and hotfix notes appear on the same release log page.
