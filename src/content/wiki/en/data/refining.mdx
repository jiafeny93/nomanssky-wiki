How to read the tables: **single-input recipes run in any refiner, including the portable one. Two-input recipes need a medium refiner, and three-input recipes (the alloys, the trade components, oxygen photosynthesis) need the large refiner.** Refiners pause — they never destroy their contents — so an idle refiner is lost income, not a lost item. Quantities below are exact game data. We deliberately omit refining times: community-measured speeds vary with refiner setup and don't reconcile across sources, while ratios are stable facts.
