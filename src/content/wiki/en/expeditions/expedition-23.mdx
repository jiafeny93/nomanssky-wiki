## Is Expedition 23 Announced Yet?

No — and in the current window, being precise about that is the whole value of a page like this. Here is exactly what is confirmed as of August 18, 2026:

- **Expedition 22: The Swarm ended around July 19, 2026**, closing a roughly eight-week run that began May 27 alongside the Swarm update (v6.4). Its three-team community war, the Hive of Glass encounter, and the Insectoid Armor set are recapped in our [Expedition 22 page](/expeditions/expedition-22).
- **No limited-time expedition is live right now.** There is nothing to log in for, no countdown on the expedition terminal, and no end date being missed while you read this.
- **Hello Games has said nothing public about Expedition 23.** No name, no window, no teaser, no reward list. Anything specific you have seen attributed to it is fabricated or fan fiction.
- **Cosmos is the confirmed next thing, and it is not an expedition.** Announced August 9, 2026 for the game's tenth anniversary, Cosmos brings direct flight to other solar systems, landings on stars, and new heat-resistant gear. The anniversary announcement attached no expedition to it. We track every confirmed change in our [Cosmos update guide](/cosmos-update/cosmos-update-guide).

For context, the 2026 rhythm so far: the Remnant update and Expedition 21 in February, the Swarm update and Expedition 22 in May, companion creature battling in June, and patches 6.36 and 6.45.1 through the summer. The full timeline is in our [2026 update guide](/guides/new-update-2026).

## When Will Expedition 23 Start? (Speculation, Clearly Labeled)

Hello Games does not publish a season calendar — every confirmed expedition date in the game's history arrived in its own announcement, usually with the start only days away. So the honest answer to "when" is **unknown**. What follows is pattern analysis, and nothing more.

Here is the recent cadence of brand-new numbered expeditions:

| Expedition | Ran | Pattern note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expedition 21: Remnant | February 11 to March 13, 2026 | Launched with the Remnant update; roughly six-week window |
| Expedition 22: The Swarm | May 27 to July 19, 2026 | Launched with the Swarm update; roughly eight-week window |

That is about a three-and-a-half-month gap between starts. Widen the lens and the rhythm holds: before those two, the November 2025 to mid-January 2026 block was wall-to-wall Redux reruns (Beachhead, Titan, Relics, Corvette, Breach), and a one-off Voyagers Redux filled August 2025. The emerging pattern is **a new numbered expedition every three to five months, with Redux reruns filling the gaps** — which is exactly the interval we are inside now.

Three scenarios for what happens next, all equally unofficial:

1. **A Cosmos-timed expedition.** The precedent is strong: the Remnant update carried Expedition 21, the Swarm update carried Expedition 22, and Cosmos is the next major update — an anniversary-sized one. An expedition launching alongside or shortly after it is the most-discussed possibility in the community. Plausible, unconfirmed.
2. **A Redux block first.** If 2026 mirrors 2025, November opens a back-to-back rerun season, and the next numbered expedition waits until early 2027. Our [Redux expeditions schedule](/expeditions/redux-expeditions-schedule) page tracks every confirmed rerun date as they appear.
3. **An off-cycle surprise.** The Voyagers Redux landing in quiet August 2025 shows Hello Games will slot content into gaps with almost no lead time. If that happens here, the gap between announcement and start could be a matter of days — one more reason to have your preparation done early.

| Pattern element | Recent precedent |
| --- | --- |
| Five phases with milestone thresholds | Both Remnant and The Swarm used the standard five-phase structure |
| A six-to-eight-week window | Remnant ran about six weeks; The Swarm about eight |

## What to Do While You Wait

A gap between expeditions is not dead time. Four things are worth doing now, all of them making the eventual run faster:

1. **Stockpile Quicksilver and Units.** Nexus missions on the Space Anomaly pay Quicksilver daily, and a healthy Units reserve lets you buy upgrades and inventory space early in a fresh expedition instead of farming for them mid-run. Our general [expedition guide](/expeditions/expedition-guide) covers exactly what to bank before a start gun fires.
2. **Learn the route before the clock starts.** Limited windows reward players who already know the flow. Read [how to start an expedition](/expeditions/how-to-start-expedition) so the save-creation step costs you nothing, and skim our [expedition fast completion](/expeditions/expedition-fast-completion) guide — the habits that put experienced runners at three or four hours transfer to every new expedition on day one.
3. **Close out Expeditions 21 and 22 now.** Both remain replayable from the expedition terminal on the Space Anomaly, so missing their rewards is not permanent. Our [Expedition 21 Remnant guide](/expeditions/expedition-21-remnant-guide) doubles as a rerun playbook, and the [Redux schedule](/expeditions/redux-expeditions-schedule) is the page to watch for a limited-time re-run with the original reward track.
4. **Play the anniversary update.** Cosmos is the biggest thing in the game right now — interstellar flight, star landings, and anniversary rewards — and its content is what an anniversary expedition would be built from. Start with the [Cosmos update guide](/cosmos-update/cosmos-update-guide).
