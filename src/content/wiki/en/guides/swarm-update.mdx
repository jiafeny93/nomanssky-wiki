It was also the game's biggest space-combat rework in years — and the community's verdict was unusually consistent: the battles are the best large-scale space combat No Man's Sky has ever delivered.

## What the Swarm Update Added

Four things define 6.4:

- **Swarmer ambushes.** Coordinated enemy ships now ambush routine flights — not set-piece missions, the regular game. Flying unupgraded through unclaimed space became genuinely dangerous overnight, which is exactly the pressure the update wanted.
- **Fleet-scale space combat.** The fighting is sustained and large-scale, with your freighter summonable into the fray. Community reaction called it the best large-scale space combat in the game's history.
- **Boardable pirate freighters.** Beat a pirate freighter and you can now board it, then either demand control (take the hull for yourself) or demand tribute (cargo and units). It is the cheapest route to a freighter in the game — the [capture walkthrough](/guides/pirate-freighter-capture) covers the full loop and its traps.
- **Expedition 22.** The seasonal expedition shipped with the update and turned its themes into an eight-week, three-team war with the Hive of Glass as the finale.

## The New Vocabulary, Defined

The update introduced its own lexicon. If a patch note, a milestone, or another player uses one of these, here is what it means:

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Swarmer / swarm ship | The coordinated enemy fighters that ambush routine flights in numbers |
| Boarding | Docking a beaten pirate freighter to claim it, instead of destroying it |
| Demand control | The boarding choice that takes the ship itself as yours |
| Demand tribute | The boarding choice that takes cargo and units and leaves the hull |
| Pirate frigates | Mercenary escort ships — hiring them before a capture stops the enemy fleet fleeing |
| Hive of Glass | Expedition 22's finale encounter, the community war's closing fight |
| Insectoid Armor set | Expedition 22's signature cosmetic reward, styled after the Swarm's chitin-and-glass enemies |

The deep mechanics behind the first five live in the [swarm battles guide](/guides/swarm-battles-guide); the expedition terms are broken down on the [Expedition 22 page](/expeditions/expedition-22).

## Expedition 22: The Three-Team War

Expedition 22 ran May 27 to July 19, 2026 — about eight weeks — and it played the update's fiction straight: three player teams fought a community war across the milestones, with the Hive of Glass encounter as the closing set-piece. Rewards followed the insect theme: the **Insectoid Armor set** as the signature cosmetic, the Swarm expedition decal, and the usual per-phase cosmetics and titles.

The window is closed, but the content is not gone. Past expeditions stay replayable from the **expedition terminal on the Space Anomaly** on any save, rewards are account-wide once unlocked, and Hello Games' fall Redux tradition means a Swarm re-run is expected around late October. The [Expedition 22 page](/expeditions/expedition-22) has the full reward list and both replay routes.

## The Patch Trail: 6.4 to 6.45.1

The Swarm shipped as 6.4 in May 2026 and was followed by six patches on a steady cadence:

- **6.41, 6.42** (May) — the first wave of fixes after launch
- **6.43, 6.44, 6.45** (June) — the June stabilization run
- **6.45.1** (June 22, 2026) — the update's final stable patch, and the version the whole 6.4 family settled on

Practical takeaway: if you played at launch and bounced off a rough edge, 6.45.1 is the version to judge the update by. The [recent updates page](/recent/) keeps the full timeline with every patch listed.
