## Interstellar Flight Between Star Systems

This is the structural change. Since launch in 2016, the only way between star systems has been the hyperdrive: charge warp cells, open the galaxy map, select a system in range, and jump. The space between stars was never traversable; it existed as backdrop.

Cosmos's announced interstellar flight feature gives you the option to fly straight to a different solar system. That single sentence rewrites the game's geography. Systems stop being sealed instances and become part of one continuous volume of space you can cross under your own power. It also raises questions the staged patch notes have not finished answering: how distance is scaled, whether free flight consumes fuel or warp energy, whether the galaxy map still plans routes for you, and how the game streams a destination that used to sit behind a loading screen. The intent is confirmed. The mechanics are arriving piece by piece.

## Landing on Stars

For a decade, stars were the one thing in No Man's Sky you categorically could not visit. They were visual elements of the skybox, and the community consensus, backed by countless threads asking the question, was that no amount of pulse flying would ever get you to one.

Cosmos makes star landings a real, confirmed feature. That is a genuine first for the game, not an iteration on an old system, which is why so little can be predicted from precedent. We know you can land on a star. We do not yet know what is there: whether stellar surfaces hold resources, anomalies, bases, story beats, or hazards beyond heat, and whether different classes of stars behave differently. Until the full patch notes land, the honest summary is that the door is open and the room behind it is still being described.

## Heat-Resistant Star Technology

The one supporting system Hello Games has confirmed is heat-resistant equipment or technology, positioned as what you will need to survive approaching and landing on a star. Specifics, names, crafting recipes, and installation slots have not been published yet.

What we can say is how a system like this typically grafts onto the game. Exosuit environmental protection already runs on a pattern: baseline hazard protection, specialized modules for extreme heat, cold, radiation, and toxicity, upgrade tiers from technology merchants, and resource-based recharging in the field. A stellar protection layer would plausibly sit above that ladder as a new tier, much like extreme planet hazards required dedicated modules when they were introduced.

| Existing protection line | Hazard it addresses | Relevance to Cosmos |
|-------------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Base hazard protection | General environmental hazards | The foundation any new tier builds on |
| Heat protection modules | Extreme heat and cold | The closest existing analog to star-heat protection |
| Radiation protection modules | Irradiated worlds | Shows how hazard-specific modules slot in |
| Toxic protection modules | Toxic worlds | Same pattern, different element |
| Protection upgrade modules | Stronger, longer-lasting shielding | Precedent for tiered stellar gear |

That table is context, not prediction. Whether star heat reuses these systems or introduces something entirely new is exactly the kind of detail the remaining patch notes should settle.

## Anniversary Cosmetic Rewards

Ten years is a milestone Hello Games clearly intends to celebrate, and coverage of the announcement points to anniversary cosmetic rewards as part of the package. No specific items have been officially itemized yet.

Precedent is a reasonable guide here. Anniversary-adjacent updates have historically distributed cosmetics through the Quicksilver shop on the Space Anomaly, expedition reward tracks, and limited-time community events. If Cosmos follows that pattern, banking Quicksilver now and keeping an eye on expedition announcements is the practical move. Until the rewards are confirmed, treat any specific item list you see as speculation.

## New Base Building Content

Base building is one of the most heavily iterated systems in No Man's Sky history, and reported details around Cosmos include new base building content once again. The scope is not yet defined: it could mean new structural parts, new cosmetic sets, anniversary-themed decorations, or functional additions.

Builders do not need to wait for certainty to benefit. Every past building expansion has drawn on the same foundations: claimed territory, harvested materials, and saved units. A tidy stockpile of common structural materials and a healthy bank balance leaves you positioned for whatever parts arrive. Watch the construction menu once the update is fully live, since past additions appeared there directly.

## New Music and the Reported Cosmos Shuffle

Audio has been part of No Man's Sky's identity since 65daysofstatic scored the original launch, and updates have repeatedly expanded the soundtrack alongside new systems. Coverage of Cosmos points to new music as well, with a track widely referenced as the Cosmos Shuffle.

What remains unclear is the form it takes: ambient tracks woven into the new stellar environments, a collectible music item for bases, or something tied to anniversary events. All three have precedent in the game's history. The safe statement is that the soundscape is growing, and the exact delivery mechanism should be confirmed as the patch notes finish rolling out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Cosmos new features are officially confirmed?

Two features are confirmed outright by Hello Games: the option to fly directly to another star system, and the ability to land on a star, supported by newly announced heat-resistant technology. Anniversary cosmetics, base building content, and new music have been reported in coverage of the update but not yet fully detailed in official patch notes.

### Will the new features change how I travel day to day?

Free interstellar flight adds an option, not a replacement. Warp jumps have been the only interstellar method for a decade, and the announcement frames flying to another system as a choice, so the hyperdrive workflow is expected to remain for efficiency. Daily routing habits should survive Cosmos intact.

### Do I need endgame gear to use the new features?

That is not yet clear. Heat-resistant equipment is confirmed to exist for star landings, but requirements have not been published. Based on how past systems rolled out, expect gating somewhere in the mid-game technology ladder rather than a hard endgame wall, and check back as details are finalized.

### Is there new story content in Cosmos?

No story additions have been confirmed in what has been published so far. Major updates often include narrative threads, and star landings in particular invite speculation about lore, but nothing specific has been announced. Treat story content as unknown rather than absent.

### How does Cosmos compare to the rest of the 2026 updates?

Cosmos is the largest item on the calendar. Remnant opened the year with Expedition 21 in February, The Swarm added large-scale space combat in May, and pet battling arrived in June before a fixing pass. Cosmos lands in August as the tentpole, changing core traversal rather than adding a parallel activity, which makes it the most consequential No Man's Sky update in some time.
