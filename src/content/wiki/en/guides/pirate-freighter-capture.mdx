For years the pirate dreadnought was a piñata: blow it up, collect the scrap, move on. The modern game gives you a better button. Beat the dreadnought, board it, and the captain surrenders the ship itself — a whole capital freighter, free, if you know the loop. This guide is the full loop: triggering the fight, forcing the surrender fast, and making the right call at the captain's table.

**Last updated: August 27, 2026.** The capture flow below reflects the long-standing dreadnought encounter as it stands in the Swarm era; exact surrender thresholds can shift patch to patch, so treat the [official patch notes](/cosmos-update/cosmos-patch-notes/) as the source of record for current numbers.

## What You Need Before the Fight

Two requirements, one strong recommendation:

1. **Own a freighter.** Pirate dreadnought battles are freighter-owner content — the encounter expects you to have skin in the game. No freighter yet? The [free freighter rescue loop](/starships/capital-freighter-farming/) is the standard on-ramp, and a cheap first hull does not disqualify you from capturing a better one later.
2. **Know where pirates live.** Outlaw systems are the reliable hunting ground — their economy data says "pirate" right on the galaxy map. Warping into pirate space with a freighter in tow is the classic way to spawn the encounter.
3. **Recommended: a combat-ready ship.** You will be disabling subsystems on a capital ship while its point defense shoots back. Shields first, weapons second — the [Swarm combat kit](/guides/swarm-battles-guide/) advice applies to dreadnought fights too.

## Step 1: Force the Surrender

A dreadnought does not die to raw hull damage — it surrenders when it can no longer fight or flee. Two subsystem targets get you there:

- **The four engines** on the rear underside. Fly under the tail, below the shields, and take them out one by one. This is the community-preferred route: predictable angles, less return fire.
- **The anti-freighter weapon systems.** Heavier work — more targets, more exposure — but it strips the ship's ability to hurt you while your own freighter tanks.

Either path ends the same way: the captain calls it, the guns go quiet, and the landing bay opens. The [dreadnought boss breakdown](/bosses/boss-fights/) covers the full encounter mechanics if you want the wave-by-wave version.

## Step 2: Board and Face the Captain

Dock in the landing bay like any freighter and walk the spine of the ship to the bridge. The captain is waiting, and the conversation ends in a choice — the entire reason this guide exists.

## The Choice: Demand Control vs. Demand Tribute

| Option | You get | You lose | Take it when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Demand control** | The freighter itself — class, slots, layout | The immediate loot payout | The hull beats what you fly now |
| **Demand tribute** | Cargo and rewards from the holds | The ship, permanently | It is a low-class hauler not worth the swap |

The honest decision rule: **compare before you commit.** When you choose to seize the freighter, the comparison screen puts its class, technology slots, and cargo capacity next to yours. Community-reported practice says you can empty the pirate holds into your inventory as part of the flow — loot first, then decide. A captured C-class with more slots than your current freighter is an upgrade; a worse hull is tribute money.

## Before You Seize: the Frigate Warning

One detail that has cost players frigates: **pirate frigates escorting the dreadnought warp away when you seize it.** If you want to hire them for your fleet, do the recruiting before you land on the flagship — talk to them in space first, then board. Seize first and the escort disappears.

## Is a Captured Pirate Freighter Actually Good?

Class and layout roll like any freighter, so the answer is "sometimes, and the price is right." What to look at on the comparison screen:

- **Slot count and layout** — the real value of any capital ship ([freighter basics](/starships/freighter-guide/)).
- **Supercharged slots**, if the hull rolled them — clustered supercharged slots are the jackpot.
- **Cargo capacity**, if you run trade loops.

Since the capture is free and repeatable — pirate systems keep spawning encounters — the practical meta is to treat every dreadnought as a lottery ticket: fight, compare, take the ship only when it beats your current hull. That cycle pairs naturally with the [S-class hunting discipline](/starships/s-class-ships/) you already use for ships.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Blind hull damage.** Shooting the hull forever does not force a surrender — subsystems do. Engines or weapons, pick a lane.
2. **Seizing without comparing.** The choice is final. The comparison screen exists; use it.
3. **Forgetting the frigates.** Hire escorts before landing, or lose them.
4. **Tribute on a good hull.** Tribute money is trivial next to a better capital ship. If the comparison screen surprises you, take the ship.

The one-sentence version: own a freighter, hunt outlaw systems, shoot the engines, board the bridge, compare, and only then decide whether the pirate's flagship is worth more than his cargo.
