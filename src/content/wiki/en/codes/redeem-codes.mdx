Every month, thousands of players search for "No Man's Sky codes" — and every month, sites happily serve them lists of codes that do not exist. Here is the short version: **No Man's Sky has no redeem codes.** Hello Games has never built a code-redemption system into the game, on any platform, in the ten years since launch.

That does not mean free items don't exist. They arrive through four real systems, and this page explains each one — what it gives, when it runs, and exactly how to claim it.

## Why do sites keep listing No Man's Sky codes?

Code culture comes from mobile and live-service games, where developers publish time-limited redeem codes for currency and cosmetics. No Man's Sky is neither: it is a buy-once game with all updates free, and its free items are delivered through in-game events rather than code redemptions.

"Code list" pages persist because the search demand is real and the topic is easy to farm. They either invent plausible-looking strings or quietly pad pages with rewards that were never codes in the first place. If a page cannot tell you *where in the game menu* a code is entered, that is your answer — there is no such menu.

## How do free items actually reach your save?

| System | What it gives | How often it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twitch Drops | Ships, multitools, cosmetics, quicksilver bundles | A few campaigns per year, usually around updates |
| Expedition rewards | Exclusive ships, multitools, armor sets, pets, emotes | Always available via replay; timed debuts |
| Nexus community missions | Quicksilver for the cosmetics shop | Weekly (weekend missions) + dailies |
| Update giveaways | Anniversary and milestone cosmetics | Around major releases, such as the Cosmos update |

## How do I claim Twitch Drops?

Twitch Drops are the closest thing NMS has to a code drop, because they come from outside the game and have a claim deadline:

1. Go to the official [Hello Games Twitch Drops page](https://cloud.nomanssky.com/twitch) and link both your Twitch account and your platform account (Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, or Switch).
2. During an active campaign, watch streams marked **Drops Enabled** for the required minutes per reward.
3. Claim each unlocked reward on Twitch — check the drop's progress bar — within **24 hours** of unlocking it. This deadline is the step most people miss.
4. Launch the game with the latest update installed and collect the items from the **Quicksilver Synthesis Companion** aboard the Space Anomaly.

<Callout type="tip" title="Watch the campaign calendar">
Campaigns are episodic — as of August 2026 the latest one has ended, and Hello Games typically opens a new campaign alongside major updates or anniversaries. Bookmark the drops page and check it after each patch. Community reports also say linking your platform account can retroactively deliver drops earned in past campaigns while unlinked, so it is worth connecting accounts even between campaigns.
</Callout>

## Which expedition rewards can I still unlock?

Every expedition that has ever run can be **replayed** from the expedition terminal aboard the Space Anomaly — you spawn a fresh expedition save, play through the milestone phases, and bank the rewards for your main saves. Hello Games also regularly re-runs expeditions as limited-time events with extra incentives, which is how newer players catch ships like older expeditions' exclusives.

If you are chasing a specific item, the walkthroughs in our [expedition guides](/expeditions/) list every phase and reward, and the fastest routes live in the [expedition completion guide](/expeditions/expedition-fast-completion/).

## What about quicksilver and weekend events?

Quicksilver buys the cosmetics inventory — ship trails, armor sets, gestures, companion parts — and it only enters the economy through the Nexus:

- **Daily Nexus missions** pay around 400 quicksilver each.
- The **weekend community mission** pays a much larger lump (about 1,800) — see the full routine in our [quicksilver farming guide](/guides/quicksilver-farming/).
- **Anniversary and milestone events**, such as the Cosmos update's 10th-anniversary rewards, add free cosmetics outright — our [anniversary rewards page](/cosmos-update/10th-anniversary-rewards/) tracks what each event gave.

## The one rule that keeps you safe

There is no menu, NPC, or website where a character string unlocks items in No Man's Sky. Any page, video, or tool promising otherwise is farming your click or your password. Everything genuinely free in this game arrives through the four systems above — and every one of them is claimed inside the game you already own.
