Over five years of seasonal events, No Man's Sky expeditions have handed out some of the most desirable items in the game — exclusive starships, organic frigates, living ships, custom multitools, and cosmetics that never return to normal play. This No Man's Sky expedition rewards list collects every expedition from 1 to 21 in one table, flags which ones have been re-run, and explains what to do when you miss one. **Last updated: August 16, 2026** — verified against the official announcements and the community wiki; rerun status beyond the dates listed below is not guaranteed to be complete.

A note on accuracy: where a standout reward could not be verified, the cell describes the theme instead of inventing item names. Milestone-by-milestone payout lists (units, nanites, blueprints) are omitted deliberately — they shift between runs, and the in-game list is always authoritative.

## How Expedition Rewards Work

Every expedition runs on its own save for a limited window, usually around six weeks. Completing enough milestones in each phase unlocks that phase's reward, claimed at the Expedition Terminal. Claimed rewards unlock account-wide and are redeemed on your other saves through the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion aboard the Space Anomaly. Anything you claimed is permanent; anything you did not claim before the window closed is gone until a rerun.

| Reward category | Examples | Carries to other saves? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicles | Starships, multitools, frigates, exocraft parts | Yes, once claimed |
| Character cosmetics | Armor sets, helmets, jetpacks, capes, gestures | Yes, once claimed |
| Base and profile cosmetics | Posters, decals, titles, banners | Yes, once claimed |
| Functional unlocks | Blueprints, upgrade modules, companions | Yes, once claimed |
| Currencies and consumables | Units, nanites, quicksilver, crafting materials | No — stay in the expedition save |

Reading the Redux column: "No rerun confirmed" means no rerun was verifiable at the time of writing, not that one will never happen. Hello Games has re-run expeditions since the format's first year, and the 2025 holiday season alone re-ran five of them back to back.

## The Rewards Veterans Still Chase

Ask a longtime player which expedition rewards matter most and the same names come up. The **Golden Vector** from Cartographers remains a flex because it has never been re-run in a confirmed window. The **Leviathan organic frigate** is the only organic frigate most players ever own. The **Wraith** living ship from Titan and the **Basilisk Crown** multitool from Relics are recent standouts, and the **Utopia Speeder** and **Starborn Runner** still turn heads at every trade outpost. From the newest runs, Expedition 21's Colossus Mechanical Legs are already the defining Colossus cosmetic.

## Missed an Expedition? Your Options

| Option | What it gets you | The catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wait for a Redux rerun | The full original reward list, occasionally with a bonus item added | Reruns are irregular; a specific expedition may wait years |
| Catch a holiday redux season | Back-to-back reruns, roughly two weeks each | Hard deadlines in the middle of the event calendar |
| Quicksilver Synthesis Companion | Anything you already unlocked but had not collected | Only works for rewards you actually earned |
| Anything else | Nothing — expedition rewards are never sold | Ignore any offer of account unlocks; they are scams |

The honest summary: patience is the only recovery mechanism. The redux system has been generous lately — five reruns in ten weeks over the 2025 holidays — but there is no published roadmap, and no reward is guaranteed to return.

## After 21: Expedition 22 and the Cosmos Question

Expedition 22, The Swarm, launched with the Swarm update in late May 2026 and ran for roughly eight weeks through the summer. Beyond that, the tenth-anniversary Cosmos update was announced in early August 2026, but Hello Games has not confirmed whether it ships a new expedition. When official details land, this list will be extended — until then, treat any Expedition 23 reward claim you see online as speculation.
